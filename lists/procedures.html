brouwer_lyddane_propagate brouwer_module Subroutine

Propagates a Cartesian state forward or backward in time using the Brouwer-Lyddane mean element theory: the input state is converted to Brouwer mean elements (short and long period terms), the secular drift is applied over the requested time span, and the resulting mean elements are converted back to an osculating Cartesian state.
